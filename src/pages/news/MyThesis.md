After really enjoying my Cyber Security and Distributed Systems courses, I decided to do my Master's thesis in the area of blockchain and distributed ledger technologies. I will be working on the BLOCKCHAIN.PT project at INESC-ID. I will be doing a world-first cross-chain interoperability research, where analytics tools will be developed to analyze the interactions between different blockchains. I am excited to contribute to this project and learn more about blockchain technology.
